From the PMI talk · Partnering with AI

What Stays With the PM

AI can help with

  • Summarization Turn long notes into a first pass
  • Drafting Status, email, outlines — still a draft
  • Brainstorming Options and angles you might miss
  • Analysis Patterns, conflicts, gaps across sources
  • Organization Tables, structure, cleanup
  • Pattern recognition What shows up again and again

PM still owns

  • Decisions AI can suggest; you decide
  • Accountability Your name is on the update
  • Quality You read it before it goes out
  • Stakeholder trust If you can’t explain it, don’t send it
  • Difficult conversations Hard talks stay human
  • Context What the room already knows — and fears
  • Prioritization What leadership hears first
AI can assist. The PM remains accountable.

Three things that do not go away

Quality & accuracy

I read it before it goes out. Confident-sounding drafts still get cut.

Decisions

AI can suggest options. I decide — and I can explain why.

People

Hard conversations, trust, and relationships stay human.

Before you send

  • Can I explain every claim in my own words?
  • Did I cut anything wrong, fluffy, or overconfident?
  • Would I put my name on this if leadership asked follow-ups?
  • Am I using an approved tool — and did I avoid pasting what I shouldn’t?

Stop signs

  • Mission-critical decisions with no human review
  • Communications you haven’t read end to end
  • Automation you can’t explain or reverse
  • Anything you wouldn’t defend in the room

If nobody in the room can explain the update, you have a trust problem — not a productivity win.
